    The Cinder Witch has stolen the last ember of the valley. Duel her card for card — spend mana, guard the flame, and douse her before yours goes out.

    How to play

    • Click a card to play it. Cards cost mana; your mana grows every round.
    • ▲ Attack deals damage. ■ Guard blocks damage until your next turn. ● Mend restores your HP. ◆ Surge gives mana or cards.
    • Keys 1–7 play a card, Enter ends your turn.
    • Reduce the Cinder Witch to 0 HP to win. If your HP hits 0, the ember fades.
